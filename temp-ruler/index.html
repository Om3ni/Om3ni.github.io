Temperatures to know

Quick estimate: °C × 2 + 30 ≈ °F. Close between about 0 and 25 °C, a few degrees high above that.

Differences to know

Superheat, subcooling, split and rise are differences. Convert them with 1.8 only. Never add or subtract 32.
