MCA / MOCP
HVAC equipment circuits · NEC Article 440

Minimum circuit ampacity and maximum overcurrent protection for equipment with a motor-compressor, and the conductor that carries it. The nameplate governs: use the calculation to check a label or for equipment that has none.

Nameplate

As printed on the unit's data plate.

Also check what the label says about the device type: many units require time-delay fuses or HACR-type breakers.

Calculate from the components

Every load on this one circuit, from the component labels or the nameplate's electrical data.

Compressors

Rated-load amps, not LRA. Where the label also gives a branch-circuit selection current (BCSC), the larger of the two is used (440.4(C)).

Other motors on this circuit

FLA from each motor. A split-system condenser usually has the outdoor fan only; the blower is on the air handler's circuit. Packaged units carry both.

Non-motor loads

Heat strips counted at 125% in MCA. Other loads (crankcase heater, controls) at 100%. Everything entered is assumed to run at the same time.
